FAQ

What is Digital Animals?

A collection of 5,000 glossy 3D liquid-glass animal NFTs (symbol DANM) on Robinhood Chain — 50 species, each with glowing eyes, translucent pink crystal flowers and neon green accents.

How many whitelist spots are there, and what do they cost?

2,500 spots — 50% of the supply. Whitelist mint is completely free (0 ETH, you only pay network gas), max 1 mint per wallet, during a 24-hour window opening Wednesday 25 November 2026 at 00:00 CET.

How do I get whitelisted?

Follow @DigitalAnimalss on X, then send a direct message to @DigitalAnimalss with your Robinhood Chain (EVM) wallet address. One entry per wallet. Entries are checked against the follow list before the whitelist locks, and the team confirms your spot in the DM.

What if I miss the whitelist?

The public mint opens Thursday 26 November 2026 at 00:00 CET at 0.001 ETH per NFT, max 10 per wallet, for the remaining 2,500 pieces.

Which chain and wallet do I need?

Robinhood Chain (chain ID 4663). Any EVM wallet works — DM the same address you will mint with. The whitelist mint itself happens on OpenSea; this page never asks you to connect a wallet or sign anything.

What is the creator fee / royalty?

5% on secondary sales on OpenSea, locked on-chain so it can't be changed or lowered by sellers.

Where do I mint?

On OpenSea, via the official collection page. The mint link will be posted on X (@DigitalAnimalss) and on the OpenSea collection page only — see the safety note below.
